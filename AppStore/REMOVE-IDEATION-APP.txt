REMOVE IDEATION APP (your company App Store Connect)
=====================================================
Required role on Ideation: Account Holder or Admin
(App Manager cannot remove apps.)

1. Open https://appstoreconnect.apple.com and sign in with IDEATION Apple ID
2. Confirm team is Ideation (NOT CMPAK LIMITED)
3. Open the app created under com.ideation.kidoApp
4. If status is Waiting for Review / In Review:
   - Remove that version from review first
5. Pricing and Availability:
   - Remove from sale in ALL countries/regions (even if never released)
6. General → App Information → Additional Information → Remove App → confirm

Notes:
- You lose the Ideation app name after removal
- Bundle ID com.ideation.kidoApp stays owned by Ideation (cannot move to CMPAK)
- Do NOT delete anything on CMPAK

Official: https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/create-an-app-record/remove-an-app
